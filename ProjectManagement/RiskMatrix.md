
# Risk Matrix - Neon Outpost

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feature creep | High | High | Freeze sprint scope and prioritize Must-have features. |
| Unity merge conflicts | Medium | High | Use feature branches and coordinate scene editing. |
| CI build failures | Medium | High | Configure Unity licensing and check build logs. |
| Unrealistic sprint scope | High | High | Implement core mechanics first and defer optional features. |
| Untested enemy AI | Medium | High | Playtest enemy chasing and fix bugs before merging. |
| Poor game performance | Medium | Medium | Profile gameplay and limit unnecessary enemy spawning. |

## Risk Management Strategy
The team will review risks during sprint meetings.
High-impact issues will be addressed before optional
features are implemented.
