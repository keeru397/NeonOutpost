
# Neon Outpost - Sprint Backlog

Sprint Duration: 2 Weeks
Team: 2 Programmers, 1 Artist, 1 Designer

## Sprint Goal
Create a playable survival shooter prototype with
player movement, enemy chasing, shooting and waves.

### US-01: Player Movement
As a player, I want to move in four directions so
that I can avoid enemies.
- AC1: WASD keys move the player.
- AC2: Movement works in all four directions.
- Points: 2
- Priority: Must

### US-02: Enemy Chase
As a player, I want enemies to chase me so that
the game feels challenging.
- AC1: Enemies detect the player within 5 units.
- AC2: Enemies move toward the player at speed 3.
- AC3: Enemies stop chasing beyond 10 units.
- Points: 3
- Priority: Must

### US-03: Enemy Waves
As a player, I want enemies to spawn in waves
so that difficulty increases.
- AC1: Enemies spawn from four directions.
- AC2: The first wave spawns four enemies.
- Points: 5
- Priority: Must

### US-04: Shooting
As a player, I want to shoot enemies so that
I can defend my base.
- AC1: Clicking fires a projectile.
- AC2: Projectiles damage enemies on contact.
- Points: 5
- Priority: Must

### US-05: Base Health
As a player, I want my base to have health
so that defending it matters.
- AC1: Enemy attacks reduce base health.
- AC2: The game ends when base health reaches zero.
- Points: 3
- Priority: Must

### US-06: Scrap Collection
As a player, I want to collect scrap so that
I can purchase upgrades.
- AC1: Defeated enemies can drop scrap.
- AC2: Collecting scrap increases the counter.
- Points: 3
- Priority: Should

### US-07: Weapon Upgrades
As a player, I want stronger weapons so that
I can survive harder waves.
- AC1: Upgrades cost 5 scrap.
- AC2: An upgrade increases weapon damage.
- Points: 5
- Priority: Should

### US-08: Visual Effects
As a player, I want shooting effects so that
combat feels more exciting.
- AC1: Shooting creates a muzzle-flash effect.
- AC2: Enemies display an effect when defeated.
- Points: 2
- Priority: Could

### US-09: Multiplayer
As a player, I want multiplayer so that
I can play with friends.
- AC1: Two players can join one session.
- AC2: Both players see synchronized movement.
- Points: 8
- Priority: Won't

## Sprint Commitment
Must-have stories: US-01 through US-05.
Should/Could stories are stretch goals.
Multiplayer is excluded from this sprint.
